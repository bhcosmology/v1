A speculative cosmological framework

Every black hole may contain a universe.

Including, perhaps, the one you are standing inside of.

The problem

General relativity predicts, with total confidence, the exact place where it stops working.

At the centre of a black hole the curvature runs away and the equations stop returning answers. That is not a calculation error: it is the theory marking where it ends.

The proposal

What if collapse doesn't end anything — and the singularity is where a new universe begins?

The energy of the collapse does not go anywhere: it is released again as expansion. What lies on the other side is a new spacetime, with its own time, sealed off from ours.

And then what?

Everyone stops at the moment of birth. Nobody asks what happens to that universe next.

This work follows it all the way: how it lives, how it dies, and where everything inside it ends up.

What this is

A speculative framework, built on established physics, taken to its logical conclusions — and stress-tested against the physicists best placed to destroy it.

The framework

The proposal is not that a black hole is a universe, but that one is born inside it — causally sealed from ours, with its own independent time, expanding from the energy of the collapse that produced it. It lives, it ages, and it ends in a state of complete causal isolation. And that final state, seen from the outside, is indistinguishable from what a black hole has always looked like: which is how everything inside it eventually returns to the universe that made it.

None of this is proven. It has no complete mathematical formalism, it predicts no numbers, and it inherits the black hole information paradox without resolving it. The fourth film is dedicated entirely to where it breaks.
